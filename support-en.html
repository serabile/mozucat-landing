Frequently asked questions

What is Mozucat? expand_more

Mozucat is a media player (IPTV client) that lets you connect to and stream content from your own IPTV service provider, using your own credentials.

Mozucat does not provide, host or distribute any content — it is only an app to access a service you are already subscribed to.

How do I add my IPTV source / credentials? expand_more

When you open the app, add your source by entering the information provided by your IPTV provider:

  • An Xtream Codes URL with username and password, or
  • An M3U playlist URL.

Your credentials are stored locally on your device. If the connection fails, double-check the URL, your credentials and that your subscription is still valid with your provider.

A stream won't play or keeps buffering expand_more

Playback quality depends on your connection and, above all, on your IPTV provider. Try the following:

  • Check your internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  • Restart the app, then your device.
  • Try another channel or piece of content — if everything is slow, the issue most likely comes from your provider's server.

If only one item is affected, contact your IPTV provider — we have no control over their servers.

How do I manage or cancel my Mozucat Pro subscription? expand_more

Mozucat Pro subscriptions are managed through your App Store or Google Play account. To cancel or change:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Mozucat.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Refund requests are made directly with the App Store or Google Play, according to their policies.

Is my data safe? expand_more

Most of your data (IPTV credentials, favorites, history) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

To learn more, see our Privacy Policy.

Which devices does Mozucat support? expand_more

Mozucat is available on iPhone and iPad, on Android (phone and tablet) as well as on compatible TVs. Download the latest version from the download page.
